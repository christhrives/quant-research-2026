# Day 4 — First Out-of-Sample Validation

**Date**: May 10, 2026 (10 days after April 30 signal)

## Context

On April 30, 2026, my Day 3 momentum strategy generated this OOS forecast:
- Holdings: CAT, AVGO, GOOGL, NVDA, GS (equal-weight 20% each)
- Holding period: May 2026
- Method: 12-month momentum, top 5, monthly rebalance

This is my first true out-of-sample test of the strategy.

## Partial Validation Results (~7 trading days)

### Individual returns (4/30 close to ~5/9 close)
- CAT:   +0.82%
- AVGO:  +3.01%
- GOOGL: +4.16%
- NVDA:  +7.83%
- GS:    +1.38%

### Aggregate
- Portfolio (equal-weight): +3.44%
- SPY benchmark:            +2.64%
- Outperformance:           +0.80% (80 bp)

### Direction
All 5 holdings positive. No drawdowns yet.

## Honest Interpretation

### Positives
- All 5 holdings moved in positive direction
- Beat SPY benchmark
- 80bp alpha in 7 days is meaningful (if sustained)

### Cautions
- Sample size too small (7 days, not enough for statistical significance)
- NVDA contributed 47% of total return - concentration risk
- May has been a rising market overall (high-beta stocks naturally rallied)
- Did not subtract trading costs (~0.1-0.2%)
- Strategy needs full-month validation, then multi-month track record

## Lessons Reinforced

1. Single data points are dangerous. One good week does not validate a strategy.
2. Top performer != Best return. CAT was rank 1 but performed worst; NVDA rank 4 but best.
3. Concentration risk is real. NVDA contributed 47% of total return.
4. Calibrated interpretation matters. Resisted the urge to declare success.
