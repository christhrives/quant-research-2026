## Key Numbers

### Universe Size
- 30 stocks + SPY benchmark = 31 total
- Time range: 2020-01-01 to 2026-04-28
- Sectors: Tech (10), Finance (5), Consumer (5), Industrial (4), Healthcare (4), Energy (2)

### Top 5 Performers (5-year)
1. NVDA - ~1100% cumulative return (AI boom winner)
2. AVGO - ~800% return
3. GE - ~400% return
4. CAT - ~400% return
5. WMT - ~250% return

### Bottom 5 Performers
1. NKE - ~-60% (worst)
2. PFE - ~-50%
3. UNH, HON, CRM - flat to slightly negative

### Critical Finding
Only 5 out of 31 stocks (16%) beat SPY over the period.

This empirically confirms the well-documented academic result that most stocks underperform the market index, even within an already-curated S&P 500 universe.

## Insights

1. Market index is hard to beat. SPY outperformed 84% of large-cap stocks.
2. AI mega-cycle dominates. Top 2 (NVDA + AVGO) are both AI/semiconductor exposure.
3. Crisis correlations spike. All stocks dropped together during 2022-09, 2023-04, 2025-09 stress events.
4. Even blue-chips can lose 60%. NKE and PFE are S&P 500 stalwarts but 5-year returns are deeply negative.
