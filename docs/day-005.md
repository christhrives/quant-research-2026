## Critical Insights

### 1. Alpha is real, but in-sample

Momentum strategy genuinely outperforms SPY across all key metrics:
- Higher returns (+4.90%)
- Better Sharpe (+0.10)
- Significantly better Sortino (+0.33)
- 41% smaller maximum drawdown
- 2.2x Calmar ratio

But this is in-sample optimization. The parameters were chosen post-hoc based on Jegadeesh-Titman convention. True out-of-sample performance will likely degrade.

### 2. Lower monthly win rate is acceptable

Strategy wins fewer months (51% vs 66%) but wins bigger when it wins. Classic momentum signature: small frequent losses, occasional big wins, positive expected value.

### 3. Crisis protection is the real value

Max drawdown of -15% vs SPY's -25% in 2022 means momentum strategy rotated out of weak stocks before the downturn. This is the practical value of momentum.

### 4. Costs matter, a lot

Day 3 preview showed 27.21% annual return. Day 5 strict (with costs) is 22.69%. The 4.52 percentage point gap is purely transaction costs.

### 5. Stock universe bias

The 30-stock universe includes major AI winners. 2020-2026 was an AI mega-cycle. Alpha is real but partly comes from universe construction.

## Math Insight: Compound vs Linear Returns

Today I deepened understanding of annualized vs cumulative returns:

- SPY: 178% cumulative over 6.25 years
- This is NOT 178/6.25 = 28.5% per year
- It's (1 + 1.78)^(1/6.25) - 1 = 17.79% per year
- The difference is compounding

Verification: 100000 * (1.1779)^6.25 = 278272
